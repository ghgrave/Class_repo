Trainer Rules

Trainers should take care of their Pokémon. Always bring enough Poké Balls before exploring. Do not enter tall grass without being prepared. Visit a Pokémon Center when your team needs help. Trainers should respect other trainers during battles. Always check your supplies before beginning a new adventure.
